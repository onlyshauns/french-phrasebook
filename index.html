Greetings & Pleasantries
💡 Always say Bonjour when entering any shop, café, or restaurant — and Au revoir when you leave. It's the #1 cultural rule.
Food & Dining
💡 The bill won't come unless you ask. Say "L'addition, s'il vous plaît" and make eye contact — don't wave or call out.
Getting Around
💡 If you're lost, start with "Excusez-moi" and always try a little French first before switching to English.

Numbers & Counting
💡 French uses a quirky system after 60 — 70 is "soixante-dix" (sixty-ten), and 80 is "quatre-vingts" (four-twenties). Worth knowing!
Emergency & Help
💡 Emergency number in France: 112 (EU standard). Police: 17. SAMU (ambulance): 15.
